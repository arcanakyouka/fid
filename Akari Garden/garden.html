A living canvas

A garden shaped like a painting

Akari's paths lead through flower beds arranged for color, form, and texture. Each season brings a new composition to notice, sketch, and enjoy together.

Composed with flowers

Gardeners think like artists as they pair bold blooms with delicate shapes, repeat colors along a path, and leave room for every plant to stand out. Warm lantern details make the displays glow at evening gatherings.

Details to discover

  • A seasonal color garden with layered flower beds
  • Small lantern-inspired sculptures along the path
  • Quiet places to sketch or study a bloom
  • An open studio table for arranging and making art
