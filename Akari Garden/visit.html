The garden is glowing

Come see what's in bloom

Visit Akari for a quiet walk, a flower-art workshop, or a lantern-lit event. You are welcome whether you come to create, observe, or simply enjoy the garden.

Plan your visit

Address: 18 Lantern Lane, Maple Grove, FL 32701

Garden hours: Tuesday–Sunday, 8:00 a.m. to Midnight

Getting here: Bike racks are available at the garden gate. The main paths are level and wide enough for a wheelchair, including a route past the seasonal floral displays.

Get in touch

Email our volunteer team at hello@akarigardencollective.org.
