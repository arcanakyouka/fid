From garden to gallery

Flowers are our medium

At Akari, a bloom can be a color study, the subject of a sketch, or the center of a shared arrangement. Our seasonal floral art celebrates the changing shapes and colors of the garden.

A changing flower palette

  • Spring: tulips, ranunculus, and delicate anemones
  • Summer: cosmos, zinnias, and bright sunflowers
  • Autumn: dahlias, chrysanthemums, and sculptural seed heads
  • Winter: evergreen textures, dried stems, and paper blooms

Make something together

Neighbors share ideas and materials in our open studio. Try a simple bouquet, draw a flower from life, or create a pressed-petal composition. No art experience is needed; curiosity is enough.
