Make something beautiful

Floral art for everyone

Akari workshops turn the garden's flowers into ideas, arrangements, and keepsakes. Sessions are relaxed and beginner-friendly; all materials are provided.

First Saturday

Color & composition

Build a flower-inspired color palette and learn how shape, scale, and contrast guide a pleasing arrangement.

Second Sunday

Botanical sketchbook

Slow down, observe a bloom, and turn its lines and details into a page for your own nature journal.

Last Friday

Lantern-lit flower arranging

Create a small seasonal arrangement for the evening table, with warm lantern light as inspiration for color and mood.

All workshops are free. Check the events page for this month's dates.
